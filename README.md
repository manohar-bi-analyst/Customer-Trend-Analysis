# Customer-Trend-Analysis
This project analyzes consumer shopping behavior for a leading retail company, with the goal of improving sales, customer satisfaction, and long‑term loyalty. Using Python, SQL, and Power BI, the dataset was cleaned, modeled, queried, and visualized to uncover actionable insights into customer segments, purchase drivers, and revenue patterns.

Business Problem:

Retail management observed shifts in purchasing patterns across demographics, product categories, and sales channels (online vs. offline). Key questions included:
Which factors (discounts, reviews, seasons, payment preferences, subscriptions) drive consumer decisions?
How can the company leverage shopping data to identify trends, improve engagement, and optimize marketing strategies

Methodology:

Data Preparation (Python) – Cleaned and transformed raw transactional data.
Data Analysis (SQL) – Structured queries to explore customer mix, loyalty, and purchase drivers.
Visualization (Power BI) – Built interactive dashboards to surface trends and comparisons.
Delivery – Packaged insights into reports, presentations, and a structured GitHub repository.

Key Insights:

Revenue by Gender: Male customers generated ~2.1× higher revenue than female customers.
Discount Impact: High‑value purchases also relied on discounts; 10 customers used discounts above the dataset average.
Product Ratings: Ratings were tightly clustered, meaning small differences significantly influenced choices.
Shipping Choice: Minimal effect on basket size.
Subscriptions: Current subscription programs did not lift average sales (59.49 vs 59.87).

Recommendations:

Segment Smarter: Design campaigns around high‑value discount users and gender‑specific revenue trends.
Improve the Offer: Focus product and review interventions on lowest‑rated items rather than treating all categories equally.
Prove Subscription Value: Enhance benefits that increase average sale or repeat purchases, not just sign‑ups.
